Progression & Tracking

  • Progression: Add load ONLY when full rep range is hit with clean form across all sets, for 2 running sessions. Form > Load.
  • Medical Stop: Stop immediately if pain, numbness, tingling, or sciatica appear. Log back sensations (0-10) after sessions.
  • Deload: Every 6th week, reduce volume by ~40% to protect the spine.
