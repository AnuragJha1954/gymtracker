Plan 1 Guidelines

  • Progression: Hit top of rep range for all sets, 2 sessions in a row → add 2.5-5kg next session.
  • Reassessment (Week 4): If weight loss is >1kg/week OR strength stalls badly, add 100-150 kcal on training days.
  • Deload: Every 6th week, reduce volume by ~40% (same exercises, fewer sets/reps) to manage fatigue.
