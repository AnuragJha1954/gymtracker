Before You Start: Critical Strategy

1. Calorie Deficit & Pace

Maintenance at 97kg with 6-day lifting + cardio is ~3,000–3,300 kcal. Track weekly average weight (not single days):

  • 0.5 – 0.75 kg/wk: Perfect pace. Retains muscle, strips body fat.
  • < 0.3 kg/wk: Reduce calories by 150–200 (carbs/fat). Keep protein fixed.
  • > 1.0 kg/wk: Add 100–150 kcal. Losing faster sacrifices muscle & strength.
* Reaching 85kg takes ~16-24 weeks. Commit to this initial 12-week block first!
2. Protein Rule

Eat 180 to 200g of protein daily (~2g per kg of target bodyweight).

Spread across 4 meals (~45-50g per meal). This is the single most important factor for preserving and building muscle while in a calorie deficit.

Tip: Whey isolate, chicken breast, eggs/whites, Greek yogurt, and paneer/soya.
3. Cardio & Fat Loss Truth

Pre-Workout Walk (25m): 10-12% incline, 4.5-5.5 km/h, conversational pace (HR 115-130 bpm).

No Spot Reduction: Belly, love handles, and chest fat drop last. Training builds the muscle underneath. Measure navel waist every 2 weeks!

On Leg Days: Reduce to 15m or lower incline if legs feel heavy.

Double Progression, Deload & Recovery Rules

Double Progression System

  1. Pick a weight where you hit the top of rep range on all sets at the indicated RIR.
  2. Each session, try to add 1 rep to the first set or two while maintaining strict form.
  3. Once every set hits the top rep range, add weight: +2.5kg for upper body, +5kg for legs, then reset to the bottom rep target.
  4. If you miss reps 2 sessions in a row: hold weight constant & rest slightly longer between sets.

Week 6 Deload & Lifestyle

  • Week 6 Deload: Cut working sets by ~50% (2 sets instead of 4). Keep weights the same. Recharges CNS & joints before Weeks 7-12.
  • Sleep 7 to 9 Hours: Sleep deprivation in a deficit directly burns muscle tissue instead of fat.
  • Daily Steps (8k-10k): Non-fatiguing NEAT that accelerates calorie burn without lifting fatigue.
  • Warm-up Protocol: 5m treadmill walk, then 2 light progressive ramp-up sets before the first heavy lift.
  • Daily Fiber (30g): Essential for gut motility & managing hunger in a deficit.

12-Week Roadmap Timeline

Weeks 1 to 3: Fast initial drop from water & glycogen. Neural adaptation makes strength climb steadily.
Weeks 4 to 8: Fat loss stabilizes at 0.5–0.75 kg/week. Noticeable visual changes begin appearing across upper torso & midsection.
Weeks 9 to 12: Waist significantly tighter at navel. Deltoids and chest achieve sculpted definition as strength is maintained or increased!
